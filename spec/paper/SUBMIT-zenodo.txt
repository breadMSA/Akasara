HOW TO TURN spec/paper/ase-tier-floor.md INTO A CITABLE DOI
===========================================================
Time: about 15 minutes. Cost: 0. No review, no endorsement, no waiting.

WHY ZENODO AND NOT ARXIV
------------------------
arXiv requires an endorsement from an existing author in the category before a
first-time submitter can post to q-bio or cs. That is a person you would have to
find and ask, and it can take weeks or never happen.

Zenodo (run by CERN) issues a DOI immediately, with no review. A DOI is the only
citation currency that both a standards committee and a court-appointed expert
recognise. Post to Zenodo now; arXiv later if an endorser ever appears, and
cross-reference the DOI when you do.


STEP 1 — MAKE THE PDF (on your PC)
----------------------------------
The file is Markdown. Zenodo will accept the .md, but a PDF is what people
actually open, so upload both.

Easiest route with no new software: open the .md in VS Code, install the
"Markdown PDF" extension, right-click in the file, choose "Markdown PDF: Export
(pdf)". It writes ase-tier-floor.pdf next to the .md.

If you would rather not install anything: paste the Markdown into
https://dillinger.io and use its Export as PDF.


STEP 2 — CREATE THE RECORD
--------------------------
1. Go to https://zenodo.org and sign in (you can log in with GitHub).
2. Click "New upload".
3. Drag in BOTH files: ase-tier-floor.pdf and ase-tier-floor.md

--------------------------------------------
Copy these values verbatim. Nothing here needs a decision from you.

    Choose  ->  "No, I need one"

    have a DOI. Pick "No, I need one" and leave the box empty; Zenodo mints the

STEP 4 — PUBLISH
----------------
Click Publish. The DOI appears immediately, in the form 10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX.

Zenodo gives you TWO DOIs:
  - a CONCEPT DOI that always points at the newest version
  - a VERSION DOI that points at exactly this version

Cite the CONCEPT DOI everywhere. It survives revisions.


STEP 5 — WIRE IT BACK IN (tell me and I will do this part)
----------------------------------------------------------
Once you have the DOI, these three edits should follow:
  - spec/README.md            : add "Cite this work: <concept DOI>"
  - spec/ASE-0.1.md  section 0: reference the paper as the rationale document
  - hub-explorer/outreach/04-pren-technical-comment.md : cite the DOI instead of
                                the bare GitHub URL, because a standards comment
                                that cites a DOI reads differently from one that
                                cites a repository

Also add the DOI to any future letter. "See github.com/..." and "See
10.5281/zenodo..." are received very differently by a committee secretariat.


WHAT THIS BUYS, PLAINLY
-----------------------
A permanent, indexed, third-party-hosted, timestamped statement of the argument
that exists whether or not anyone answers an email. Outreach letters are a push
channel and they have not worked. This is a pull channel: it is what is found by
someone who goes looking at the moment they need it — a committee member
drafting a clause, or a lawyer who has just been handed a neural-device record
and needs to know whether it can be challenged.
